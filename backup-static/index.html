Logistics Coverage

International Shipping Corridors

Middle East Trade Lane 🇦🇪

Average Ocean Transit: 4 - 7 Days (FCL)

Sailing directly from Nhava Sheva (JNPT) or Mundra Port to Jebel Ali (Dubai), Jeddah, and Dammam. We coordinate full customs clearance for dry food ingredients.

  • Onion Powder & Dehydrated Garlic
  • Premium CTC Tea & Coffee Blends
  • Organic Cotton Yarns

European Trade Lane 🇳🇱

Average Ocean Transit: 18 - 22 Days (FCL)

Connecting Western Europe via Rotterdam, Hamburg, and Antwerp. Full compliance with European Food Safety standards (EFSA) and phytosanitary screening.

  • Dehydrated Onion Flakes (Hygienic Mesh)
  • Absorbent Cotton & Finished Apparel Fabric
  • Artisanal Handicrafts

Transatlantic Trade Lane 🇺🇸

Average Ocean Transit: 25 - 28 Days (FCL)

Shipping to New York, Savannah, and Los Angeles ports. US FDA compliant documentation provided for agricultural cargo loads.

  • Organic Ground Spices (Turmeric, Chilli)
  • Engineering fasteners & industrial castings
  • Commercial grade chemicals

East Asian Trade Lane 🇯🇵

Average Ocean Transit: 12 - 15 Days (FCL)

Routes serving Osaka, Tokyo, Shanghai, and Singapore. Streamlined customs booking with priority B2B shipping lines.

  • Organic Cotton Bales
  • Industrial Fasteners & Specialty Alloys
  • Dehydrated spice blends

East African Trade Lane 🇰🇪

Average Ocean Transit: 8 - 11 Days (FCL)

Sailing to Mombasa, Dar es Salaam, and Durban ports. Providing flexible payment arrangements for emerging agricultural markets.

  • Bulk Food Ingredients
  • Home textiles and woven canvas
  • Agricultural equipment components
